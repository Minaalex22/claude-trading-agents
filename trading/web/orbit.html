Orbit portfolio

Five strategy arms trade one account, each in its own 20% sleeve. The edge is in the mix, not one arm's win rate: arms that don't lose at the same time, and sizes that shrink when an arm goes cold (0.35x) and grow when it runs hot (1.45x). Every trade here is a real desk trade on real prices, costs taken off, practice-quota trades left out.
