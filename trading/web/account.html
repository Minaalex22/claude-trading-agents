ATASorder flow from the indicator on your computer

  1. Tap Connect ATAS and copy the line for your computer.
  2. Paste it in Terminal (Mac) or PowerShell (Windows) and press Enter. It installs the add-on and links it to this app.
  3. In ATAS open a chart, Indicators, add JarvisFeed (ATS Matrix), OK. Nothing to type. This box turns LIVE.
It never asks for your ATAS password and only sends data, never orders. ATAS must be open for data to flow.
