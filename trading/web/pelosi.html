Shares desk · Congress trades (STOCK Act)

Pelosi trader

Follows the trades Nancy Pelosi and other House members must disclose. Every trade has two dates: the day they traded and the day it was announced (the law allows up to 45 days). Each one says BUY or SELL, and the amount is a range, never an exact number. The desk buys on paper the first trading day after the announcement, holds about 3 months, and sells early if the member's sale is announced.
